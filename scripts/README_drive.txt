Jordanian School Textbooks 2025/2026 - الكتب المدرسية الأردنية
=================================================================

What this is
------------
The official Jordanian school textbooks (PDF) for Grades 1-12, published by
the National Center for Curriculum Development (NCCD - المركز الوطني لتطوير المناهج).
Included: Student Books and Activity/Exercise Books, both semesters, every subject.

How it is organized
-------------------
  Grade 01 - الصف الأول/
    Mathematics - الرياضيات/
      Semester 1 - الفصل الأول/
        Student Book - كتاب الطالب.pdf
        Activity Book - كتاب الأنشطة والتمارين.pdf
      Semester 2 - الفصل الثاني/
  ...
  Grade 11 Academic - الصف الحادي عشر - المسار الأكاديمي/
  Grade 12 Academic - الصف الثاني عشر - المسار الأكاديمي/

INDEX.csv (next to this file, when present) lists every book with its page
count and the official NCCD link it came from.

Source
------
All books are © NCCD and are published free of charge on the official site:
  https://www.nccd.gov.jo
The official site always has the latest editions. This collection follows the
book list NCCD published on its site as of May 2026 (2025/2026 school year,
including the new 2026 Grade 11/12 mathematics editions). The files are the
official NCCD PDFs, retrieved through the Internet Archive because nccd.gov.jo
only answers inside Jordan. Any book that came from a different source is
marked in INDEX.csv (column "source").
