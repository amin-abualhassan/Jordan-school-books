## Jordanian school textbooks — Grades 1–12 (NCCD, 2025/2026)

Official student books, activity books and supporting worksheets from the National Center for Curriculum Development (المركز الوطني لتطوير المناهج), one zip per grade.

**To put them on Google Drive**
1. Download the zips below (Grade-01.zip … Grade-12-Academic.zip). A computer is easiest: the total is about 13 GB.
2. Extract them all into the same place. They merge into a single folder, `Jordan-School-Books-2025-2026`.
3. Drag that folder into drive.google.com, then Share → General access: *Anyone with the link* → Viewer.

`INDEX.csv` lists every book (grade, subject, semester, pages, and where the file came from). The official, always-current source is https://www.nccd.gov.jo (it only opens from inside Jordan).
